# Setting Financial Goals

## Make goals specific
A useful goal has a purpose, an amount and a date. "Save 120,000 for a laptop and travel in 12 months" works better than "save more". Divide the target by the number of months to find the monthly amount you need.

## Short, medium and long-term goals
Short-term goals (under a year) include an emergency fund starter, a gadget or a trip. Medium-term goals (one to five years) include a vehicle down payment or education fees. Long-term goals (five years or more) include a home or retirement. Match the place you keep the money to the timeline: safer, accessible accounts for short goals.

## Pay yourself first
Move the goal amount into a separate savings account as soon as income arrives, before you spend on anything else. Automating the transfer removes the need for willpower.

## Track progress and adjust
Review goals monthly. If you are behind, you can raise the monthly amount, extend the deadline or reduce the target. Celebrate milestones such as 25%, 50% and 75% to stay motivated.

## Prioritise
When you have several goals, rank them. A common order is: a small starter emergency fund, high-interest debt repayment, a full emergency fund, then other goals and investing.
