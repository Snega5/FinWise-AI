# Insurance and Financial Protection

## Why insurance matters
Insurance transfers the risk of a large, unpredictable cost to an insurer in exchange for a regular premium. It protects your savings from being wiped out by a medical emergency, an accident or the loss of income.

## Health insurance
A single hospital stay can cost more than many people save in a year. Health insurance covers major medical costs. Check the sum insured, the waiting periods, room-rent limits and the list of network hospitals. Employer cover is useful but may end if you change jobs, so consider a personal policy as well.

## Term life insurance
Term insurance pays your dependants a lump sum if you pass away during the policy term. It is usually the cheapest way to get a large cover. It is mainly useful if other people rely on your income. Avoid mixing insurance and investment in one product unless you clearly understand the costs.

## Other cover
Vehicle insurance is legally required in many places. Home or contents insurance can protect your belongings. Review your cover once a year and after major life changes such as marriage, a new child or a new home.

## Premiums in your budget
Insurance premiums are a fixed need, not a want. Include them in your essential spending so they are paid on time, because a lapsed policy can leave you unprotected when you need it.
